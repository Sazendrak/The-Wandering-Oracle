Class Features

PL 21: Proficiencies​

When you take this prestige class, choose two additional skills from the Warlock skill list. You gain proficiency in both.

PL 21: Ascended Pact Magic​

Your amount of available pact slots increases per the Morelock Table.

PL 22: Improved Magical Cunning​

You can now Expend an action, bonus action and all your movement to use Magical cunning.

PL 23: Patron​

Your eldritch mastery has grown so strong you can now have 2 different patrons without conflict, You may choose the same warlock patron, or you may choose a new one. At PL 23, PL 26, PL 31 and PL37, you gain more features from your warlock patron.

PL 24: Ability Score Improvement​

At PL 24, 28, 32, 36, and 39, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or two ability scores by 1. As normal, you cannot increase a score above 20 (unless another feature allows it). You may select a Feat instead of an ASI.

PL 25: Journeyman Pact Magic​

Your Pact Slots increase in power. All of your available Warlock Pact Slots are now 6th level. You do not learn any new 6th-level spells to cast with these slots; they can only be used to upcast the Warlock spells you know of 5th level or lower, or to cast the specific 6th-level Warlock spells you have already learned through your Mystic Arcanum feature.

PL 29: Ascendant Contact Patron​

Your connection to your patron transcends the boundaries of the planes. When you use your Warlock Contact Patron feature to cast Contact Other Plane, the casting time is reduced to 1 Action, and you do not need to speak aloud to ask your questions.

Additionally, once per Long Rest, you can cast the Gate spell without expending a Pact Slot or requiring material components. When cast in this way, the portal can only open to the home domain of your Patron, allowing you to step into their realm.

PL31: Heightened Mystic Arcunam​

Choose another level 6 Warlock spell as this arcanum.

You can cast your 6th level arcanum spell twice without expending a spell slot, and you must finish a Long Rest before you can cast it in this way again.

As shown in the Morelock Features table, you gain another Warlock spell of your choice that can be cast in this way when you reach Warlock levels 33 (level 7 spell), 35 (level 8 spell), and 37 (level 9 spell), these can be cast as arcanums twice per long rest. You regain all uses of your Mystic Arcanum when you finish a Long Rest.

Whenever you gain a Warlock level, you can replace one of your arcanum spells with another Warlock spell of the same level.

PL 35: Expert Pact Magic​

Your soul acts as a massive conduit for your patron's magic. All of your Warlock Pact Slots are now 7th level. As before, you do not learn new 7th-level spells for these slots; they may only be used to upcast your known Warlock spells of 6th level or lower, or to cast the 7th-level Warlock spells you have learned through your Mystic Arcanum feature.

PL 40: Pact Magic Mastery​

Your mind has expanded to withstand the cosmic weight of your patron's true power, allowing you to fracture your focus without breaking it. You can now concentrate on two Warlock spells simultaneously, provided both spells were cast using your Pact Slots.

Wielding this dual magic is a volatile and taxing endeavor. If you take damage while concentrating on two spells and are forced to make a Constitution saving throw to maintain Concentration, you must make the roll with Disadvantage. If you fail the saving throw, the magic violently unravels, and you lose Concentration on both spells simultaneously.

This ceremony can be performed on a short or long rest, whereupon the previous garbs disappear. The garbs fade away when you die. You cannot benefit from the garbs or it's invocations while wearing other armor.

| | Weaver’s Birthright | 1 | Prerequisites: Pact of the Garb

While wearing your garb, you can use an action to transform your garb’s appearance to any form of mundane clothing you wish. This does not change the garb’s statistics or properties.

Choose one of your known Warlock cantrips that deals damage. You can add your Charisma modifier to that spell’s damage rolls.

Repeatable. You can gain this invocation more than once. Each time you do so, choose a different eligible cantrip.

Repeatable. You can gain this invocation more than once. Each time you do so, choose a different Origin feat.

| | Mask of Many Faces | 2 | You can cast Disguise Self without expending a spell slot. | | Misty Visions | 2 | You can cast Silent Image without expending a spell slot. | | Otherworldly Leap | 2 | You can cast Jump on yourself without expending a spell slot. | | Repelling Blast | 2 | Prerequisite: a Warlock Cantrip That Deals Damage via an Attack Roll

Choose one of your known Warlock cantrips that requires an attack roll. When you hit a Large or smaller creature with that cantrip, you can push the creature up to 10 feet straight away from you.

Repeatable. You can gain this invocation more than once. Each time you do so, choose a different eligible cantrip.

| | Ascendant Step | 5 | You can cast Levitate on yourself without expending a spell slot. | | Eldritch Smite | 5 | Prerequisite: Pact of the Blade Invocation

Once per turn when you hit a creature with your pact weapon, you can expend a Pact Magic spell slot to deal an extra 1d8 Force damage to the target, plus another 1d8 per level of the spell slot, and you can give the target the Prone condition if it is Huge or smaller.

| | Gaze of Two Minds | 5 | You can use a Bonus Action to touch a willing creature and perceive through its senses until the end of your next turn. As long as the creature is on the same plane of existence as you, you can take a Bonus Action on subsequent turns to maintain this connection, extending the duration until the end of your next turn. The connection ends if you don’t maintain it in this way.

While perceiving through the other creature’s senses, you benefit from any special senses possessed by that creature, and you can cast spells as if you were in your space or the other creature’s space if the two of you are within 60 feet of each other.

| | Gift of the Depths | 5 | You can breathe underwater, and you gain a Swim Speed equal to your Speed.

You can also cast Water Breathing once without expending a spell slot. You regain the ability to cast it in this way again when you finish a Long Rest.

| | Investment of the Chain Master | 5 | Prerequisite: Pact of the Chain Invocation

When you cast Find Familiar, you infuse the summoned familiar with a measure of your eldritch power, granting the creature the following benefits.

Aerial or Aquatic. The familiar gains either a Fly Speed or a Swim Speed (your choice) of 40 feet.

Quick Attack. As a Bonus Action, you can command the familiar to take the Attack action.

Necrotic or Radiant Damage. Whenever the familiar deals Bludgeoning, Piercing, or Slashing damage, you can make it deal Necrotic or Radiant damage instead.

Your Save DC. If the familiar forces a creature to make a saving throw, it uses your spell save DC.

Resistance. When the familiar takes damage, you can take a Reaction to grant it Resistance against that damage.

| | Master of Myriad Forms | 5 | You can cast Alter Self without expending a spell slot. | | One with Shadows | 5 | While you’re in an area of Dim Light or Darkness, you can cast Invisibility on yourself without expending a spell slot. | | Thirsting Blade | 5 | Prerequisite: Pact of the Blade Invocation

You gain the Extra Attack feature for your pact weapon only. With that feature, you can attack twice with the weapon instead of once when you take the Attack action on your turn.

| | Eldritch Defense | 5 | Prerequisites: Pact of the Garb

By spending an hour in ritual with any shield, you bind it to your eldritch garb, you can only bind a single shield to your garbs. You gain proficiency with shields and retain you garb AC, while wielding a shield.

| | Eldritch Armor | 5 | Prerequisites: Pact of the Garb

Choose two damage types. You are resistant to those damage types while you are wearing your garb. At the end of a long rest, you may change the damage types you chose.

| | Whispers of the Grave | 7 | You can cast Speak with Dead without expending a spell slot. | | Gift of the Protectors | 9 | Prerequisite: Pact of the Tome Invocation

A new page appears in your Book of Shadows when you conjure it. With your permission, a creature can take an action to write its name on that page, which can contain a number of names equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of one name).

When any creature whose name is on the page is reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright, the creature magically drops to 1 Hit Point instead. Once this magic is triggered, no creature can benefit from it until you finish a Long Rest.

As a Magic action, you can erase a name on the page by touching it.

| | Lifedrinker | 9 | Prerequisite: Pact of the Blade Invocation

Once per turn when you hit a creature with your pact weapon, you can deal an extra 1d6 Necrotic, Psychic, or Radiant damage (your choice) to the creature, and you can expend one of your Hit Point Dice to roll it and regain a number of Hit Points equal to the roll plus your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1 Hit Point).

| | Visions of Distant Realms | 9 | You can cast Arcane Eye without expending a spell slot. | | Devouring Blade | 12 | Prerequisite: Thirsting Blade Invocation

The Extra Attack of your Thirsting Blade invocation confers two extra attacks rather than one.

| | Insurmountable Linens | 12 | Prerequisites:Pact of the Garb

When you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a saving throw to take only half damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw.

| | Witch Sight | 15 | You have Truesight with a range of 30 feet. | | Eldritch Annihilation | 22 | Prerequisite*:** Eldritch Smite invocation*

When you expend a Pact Magic spell slot to use your Eldritch Smite, the localized impact ruptures space. You can choose to unleash a blast of raw magic. All creatures of your choice within 15 feet of the original target must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Spell Save DC. They take Force damage equal to the smite's extra damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. Huge or smaller creatures that fail the save also gain the Prone condition.

| | Legion of the Chain Master | 22 | Prerequisite: Pact of the Chain

When you cast Find Familiar, you can summon two familiars instead of one. They can be of the same or different forms. When you take the Attack action and forgo one of your attacks, you can command both familiars to make one attack of their own with their Reactions.

| | Piercing Gaze of the Elder | 22 | Prerequisite: Devil's Sight invocation.

Your Devil's Sight range increases from 120 feet to 300 feet. Additionally, as a Bonus Action, you can focus your vision to see through up to 5 feet of solid, non-magical matter for 1 minute. You can use this piercing vision a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier per Long Rest.

| | Eldritch Casting | 25 | Prerequisite: a Warlock spell That Deals Damage

Choose one of your known Warlock spells that deals damage and has a range of 10+ feet. When you cast that spell with a pact slot, its range increases by a number of feet equal to 30 times your Morelock level

| | Cataclysmic Repulsion | 25 | Prerequisite: Repelling Blast invocation.

When you use your Repelling Blast invocation to push a creature, you can now push them up to 30 feet straight away from you. If the target is pushed into an obstacle or another creature, they both take Force damage equal to your Charisma modifier. You can now affect creatures of any size.

| | Chronicles of the Ancients | 25 | Prerequisite: Pact of the Tome

Your Book of Shadows expands to absorb grander magic. Choose two spells of 6th level or lower from any class's spell list. These spells are added to your Book of Shadows, you always have them prepared, and they function as Warlock spells for you. You can cast them using your Pact Slots.

| | Mantle of the Void | 25 | Prerequisite: Eldritch Armor invocation

Your protective garments absorb the harshest of elements. When you choose your damage types for the Eldritch Armor invocation at the end of a Long Rest, you may now choose three damage types to resist. You may choose to elevate one of these resistances into an absolute Immunity.

| | Flight of the Beyond | 25 | Prerequisite: Ascendant Step invocation.

As an action you gain a magical Fly Speed equal to your walking speed, and you can hover for up to 10 minutes

| | Eldritch Apex | 25 | Prerequisite: Agonizing Blast invocation

The foundational magic granted by your patron reaches its absolute zenith. Whenever you cast a Warlock cantrip that is currently benefiting from your Agonizing Blast invocation, its power is magnified beyond mortal limits. If the cantrip normally scales its damage at higher levels (such as 5th, 11th, and 17th levels), it now rolls one additional damage die of its normal type.

Eldritch Blast Exception: Eldritch Blast interacts with this power differently. You can fire an additional beam instead of getting an additional damage die.

| | Eldritch Vitality | 27 | Prerequisite: Fiendish Vigor invocation.

The survival instincts granted by your patron amplify. When you cast False Life on yourself without expending a pact slot using this invocation, the spell is automatically cast at 5th level, automatically granting you 28 Temporary Hit Points.

| | Void Walker | 27 | Prerequisite: One with Shadows invocation.

When you use One with Shadows to cast Invisibility on yourself in Dim Light or Darkness, the spell no longer requires Concentration. The invisibility still ends early if you attack, cast a spell, or enter an area of Bright Light.

| | Ruinous Blade | 27 | Prerequisite:Lifedrinker invocation

The necrotic, psychic, or radiant damage dealt by your Lifedrinker invocation ignores resistance, and treats immunity as resistance. Additionally, when you expend a Hit Point Die to regain health via Lifedrinker, you double the total amount of Hit Points restored.

| | Eldritch Behemoth | 29 | Prerequisite: Investment of the Chain Master invocation

Your familiar's form swells with overwhelming eldritch energy. As a Bonus Action, you can enlarge one of your familiars to Large or Huge size for 1 minute. While enlarged, it gains Temporary Hit Points equal to five times your Warlock level, and its attacks deal an additional 3d8 damage of its normal type. You can use this feature once per Long Rest.

| | Master of the Unknowable Form | 29 | Prerequisite: Master of Myriad Forms invocation.

When you cast Alter Self using this invocation, the spell no longer requires Concentration. Additionally, once per Long Rest, you can cast Polymorph targeting only yourself without expending a spell slot

| | Gift of the Undying | 29 | Prerequisite: Gift of the Protectors invocation

When a creature whose name is written in your Book of Shadows is reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright, they do not drop to 1 Hit Point. Instead, they magically regain Hit Points equal to half their hit point maximum. Additionally, this magic can now be triggered twice per Long Rest, though no single creature can benefit from it more than once per Long Rest

| | Patron's Emissary | 32 | Prerequisite: Pact of the Chain

If your familiar is reduced to 0 Hit Points, it violently explodes in a burst of raw magic. All enemies within 30 feet must make a Dexterity saving throw against your Spell Save DC. They take Force damage equal to 1d10 per your Morelock level on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. Once per Long Rest, you can immediately cast Find Familiar as a Reaction to this explosion without expending a spell slot or material components.

| | Aegis of the Outer Gods | 32 | Prerequisite: Insurmountable Linens invocation

When you use Insurmountable Linens to take no damage on a successful saving throw, you can use your Reaction to aggressively reflect the magic. The creature that forced you to make the saving throw must make a Charisma saving throw against your Spell Save DC. On a failed save, it takes Force damage equal to half the damage you would have originally taken. On a successful save, it takes half of that reflected damage.

| | Omnipresent Vision | 35 | Prerequisite: Witch Sight invocation.

Your Truesight from the Witch Sight invocation extends from 30 feet to 60 feet. Furthermore, you are permanently immune to the Blinded condition.

| | Apocalyptic Blade | 35 | Prerequisite: Devouring Blade invocation

Your mastery over martial combat transcends mortal speed. When you take the Attack action on your turn to attack with your pact weapon, you can make one additional attack with it as a Bonus Action.

| | Weaver's Ascendancy | 35 | Prerequisite: Pact of the Garb

Your garb reaches its ultimate, perfected form. The base AC of your garb becomes 12 + your Charisma modifier + your Dexterity modifier. Additionally, the eldritch nature of the threads makes you immune to the Grappled, Restrained, and Paralyzed conditions while wearing the garb.

| | Omniscient Grimoire | 35 | Level Pact of the Tome

Your mind operates on dual planes simultaneously. While your Book of Shadows is on your person, you have Advantage on all saving throws against spells and magical effects. Furthermore, the infinite pages of your grimoire contain the blueprints of all magic across the multiverse. Once per Long Rest, you can consult your Book of Shadows to cast any spell of 5th level or lower from any class's spell list. When you cast a spell in this way, it is automatically cast at the level of your Warlock Pact Slots (such as 7th level) without expending a spell slot or requiring material components.
