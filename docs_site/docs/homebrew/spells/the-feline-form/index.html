The Feline Form

Coiled and silent. Retractable claws, a spine that stores tension like a drawn bow, eyes that resolve detail in near-total dark. Feline in build, though not necessarily in kind.

Baseline — Silent Predator​

  • Night Eyes: You gain Darkvision out to 120 feet, or extend your existing Darkvision by 60 feet.
  • Soundless: You have a Climb Speed equal to your Speed and can move across ceilings and vertical surfaces without a check. You can take the Hide action as a Bonus Action even when only lightly obscured, and your first melee attack against a creature that cannot see you deals additional damage equal to your Martial Arts die.

Signature — Pounce (2 Primal Points)​

  • Spring: As part of your Attack action, leap up to 30 feet to a space you can see and make a melee attack on landing.
  • First Blood: If you were Hidden or the target hasn't acted yet this combat, that strike is an automatic critical hit, and the target must succeed on a Strength saving throw against your save DC or fall Prone.

PL 25 — Feline Branches​

Choose one: The Ambusher, The Shadow, or The Ripper.

The Ambusher​

Opener — Patient Blood: You have advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn yet this combat, and against any creature that can't see you.

PL 30 — choose one:

  • Held Breath: Your Stealth checks cannot be reduced below your passive Wisdom (Perception) score, and moving at full Speed imposes no penalty on them.
  • Coiled: You automatically win Initiative against any creature that has not detected you, acting before the first round begins.

PL 35 — choose one:

  • Opening Salvo: The first spell you cast in each combat is treated as though cast with a spell slot one level higher, and creatures that have not yet taken a turn have disadvantage on saving throws against it.
  • Second Spring: If your Pounce reduces a creature to 0 Hit Points, you can immediately Pounce again at no cost.

PL 40 — Capstone: The First and Last Moment: Once per Short or Long Rest, at the start of a combat in which no enemy has detected you, you take a free turn before Initiative is rolled. Pounce used on that turn costs nothing.

The Shadow​

Opener — Vanish: As a Bonus Action, spend 1 Primal Point to become Invisible until the end of your next turn or until you deal damage. When you end this invisibility by attacking, that attack scores a critical hit on a roll of 19–20.

PL 30 — choose one:

  • Nowhere and Everywhere: While Invisible, your Speed doubles and you leave no tracks, scent, or trace of passage.
  • Blur the Line: When a creature misses you with an attack, you can spend 1 Primal Point to immediately become Invisible until the end of your next turn.

PL 35 — choose one:

  • Gone Again: Once per turn, when you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points, you immediately become Invisible until the end of your next turn without spending a Primal Point.
  • Veiled Casting: You can cast spells while Invisible without ending the invisibility, provided the spell deals no damage. Spells cast this way need no Verbal component.

PL 40 — Capstone: Never There At All: Vanish costs no Primal Point and you can use it as a Free Action. Dealing damage no longer ends it; instead it ends at the start of your second turn after you deal damage, and you can renew it freely once it has ended.

The Ripper​

Opener — Severing Claws: When you score a critical hit with a melee attack, the target's Hit Point maximum is reduced by the damage dealt until it finishes a Long Rest, and it cannot regain Hit Points until the end of your next turn.

PL 30 — choose one:

  • Wide Wound: Your melee attacks score critical hits on a roll of 19–20.
  • Bleed: A creature whose Hit Point maximum you reduced takes damage equal to your Martial Arts die at the start of each of its turns until it finishes a Short or Long Rest.

PL 35 — choose one:

  • Tear: On a critical hit, roll your Martial Arts dice three times instead of twice.
  • Withering Magic: A creature whose Hit Point maximum you reduced has disadvantage on saving throws against your spells, and takes an extra Martial Arts die of damage from them.

PL 40 — Capstone: Nothing Heals: A creature can succeed on a Constitution saving throw against your save DC at the end of each of its turns to end the Hit Point maximum reduction from Severing Claws; until it does, the reduction persists through rests. A creature reduced below half its original maximum this way has the Frightened condition with respect to you.
