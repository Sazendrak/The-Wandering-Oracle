The Hollow Crown

Great Old One. Rule is not persuasion. Rule is simply what is true.

At PL 25 you choose a Sovereignty; PL 29 and 33 branch within it; PL 37 gives your Sovereignty's capstone. Pact Magic slots grow at PL 21, 29, and 37 only.

Order Disciplines​

Your Order grants these two disciplines at PL 21. They don't count against the number of disciplines you know, and no other Order can learn them.

Voice of the Deep​

○ Focus. You can speak to and be understood by any creature, and you know when one knowingly lies to you.

● Suggestion Unspoken (4 psi) — Bonus Action. One creature within 60 ft makes a Wisdom save or accepts one reasonable statement you make as true until given clear evidence otherwise.

●● Chorus (9 psi) — Bonus Action. Up to three creatures within 60 ft make Wisdom saves. Each that fails is Charmed by you for 1 minute.

Unreason​

○ Focus. Creatures that start their turn within 10 ft of you have Disadvantage on Intelligence checks.

● Cognitive Static (3 psi) — Rider. Once per turn when you damage a creature, it can't take Reactions until the end of its next turn.

●●● Nothing Follows (14 psi) — Magic action. Each enemy in a 30-ft cone makes an Intelligence save. On a failure a creature takes 10d10 Psychic damage and, on its next turn, attacks the creature nearest to it, ally or not.

PL 21: Crown of Whispers​

+1 Pact Magic slot. You know the surface thoughts of any creature within 60 ft you can see with Intelligence 3 or higher. As a Bonus Action, one such creature makes a Wisdom save, taking 3d8 Psychic damage and having Disadvantage on its next attack roll on a failure.

PL 25: Choose a Sovereignty​

The Whisper​

Control. You can control two creatures at once with Dominate Impulse, and dominated creatures act on your initiative.

The Dread​

Terror in melee. You can manifest a weapon of solidified thought as a Bonus Action, whether or not you have the Pact of the Blade invocation. It counts as your pact weapon for any feature referencing one, is magical, deals Psychic damage, and uses your Psionic Ability modifier for attack and damage rolls. When you hit a creature with it, that creature makes a Wisdom save or is Frightened of you until the end of its next turn.

The Alien​

Unreality. Once per turn when you damage a creature, you can teleport it 15 ft to an unoccupied space you can see.

PL 29: Choose a Technique (+1 Pact Magic slot)​

Whisper​

  • Puppet Strings. A creature you dominate can use its Reaction at your command, and you can see and hear through its senses.
  • Hollow Regent. Creatures you dominate gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your Prestige Level and add your Psionic Ability modifier to their attack rolls.

Dread​

  • Feast of Fear. Creatures Frightened of you take Psychic damage equal to your Proficiency Bonus at the start of each of their turns. The first time this damage is dealt each round, you gain that many Temporary Hit Points.
  • Eldritch Strike. Once per turn when you hit with your pact weapon, you can expend a Pact Magic slot to deal an extra 2d8 Psychic damage per slot level. The target has Disadvantage on its next saving throw against you.

Alien​

  • Unspeakable Truth. As a Bonus Action, one creature within 60 ft makes an Intelligence save, taking 6d8 Psychic damage and being Stunned until the end of your next turn on a failure. Usable a number of times per Long Rest equal to your Proficiency Bonus.
  • Nonsense Geometry. Attacks against you from more than 30 ft have Disadvantage, and you can teleport 15 ft as part of any move.

PL 33: Choose a Mastery​

Whisper​

  • Whispers Made Law. Once per turn when a creature fails a saving throw against one of your disciplines, it takes Psychic damage equal to your Prestige Level.
  • No Word Refused. A creature that has failed a save against Dominate Impulse in this encounter has Disadvantage on all further saves against you.

Dread​

  • Terror Incarnate. Creatures Frightened of you can't take Reactions and have Disadvantage on all attack rolls.
  • Warped Blade. Your pact weapon ignores Resistance and scores Critical Hits on a 19 or 20. When you reduce a creature to 0 Hit Points with it, gain Temporary Hit Points equal to your Prestige Level.

Alien​

  • Thin the Veil. Once per turn, force a creature you damage to make an Intelligence save or be banished to a pocket of the Far Realm until the end of your next turn.
  • Wrong Angles. Enemies within 30 ft treat the area as difficult terrain and have Disadvantage on Intelligence and Wisdom saves.

PL 37: Sovereignty Capstone (+1 Pact Magic slot)​

Whisper — Throne of the Far Realm. Once per Long Rest as a Magic action, each enemy within 60 ft that can see you makes a Wisdom save or is Incapacitated for 1 minute, repeating at the end of each of its turns. While a creature is Incapacitated this way, you can spend 4 psi as a Bonus Action to have it take one action of your choosing.

Dread — The Unbearable. Once per Long Rest as a Bonus Action, for 1 minute: each enemy within 30 ft that can see you makes a Wisdom save at the start of its turn or is Frightened of you, you make one additional attack whenever you take the Attack action, and your pact weapon deals an extra 2d10 Psychic damage against Frightened creatures.

Alien — Apotheosis of the Outside. Once per Long Rest as a Bonus Action, for 1 minute your creature type becomes Aberration, you gain a flying speed of 60 ft and Resistance to all damage, and each enemy starting its turn within 30 ft makes an Intelligence save or takes 6d10 Psychic damage.
