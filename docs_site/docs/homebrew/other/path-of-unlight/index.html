Path of Unlight

Path of Unlight​

Unleash your inner light as blazing fury

Barbarians infused with the power of Unlight embrace uncontrollable magic in exchange for incredible physical prowess. The Unlight burning inside them illuminates the darkness while burning and blinding the barbarian's enemies.

Most barbarians on this path were first exposed to Unlight in the Underdark, sometimes referred to as the Underlight. The magic of Unlight is contagious and spreads in unpredictable ways. It infects people only briefly exposed to it and occasionally manifests in individuals who have never been to the Underdark.

Features:

  • Unlight Manifestation
  • 3rd Level - Radiant Rage
  • 6th Level - Unlight Revelation
  • 10th Level - Infectious Unlight
  • 10th Level - Harbinger of Unlight
  • 14th Level - Brilliant Rage

Unlight Manifestation​

Barbarians on the Path of Unlight manifest the radiant energy of Unlight when they rage, but this energy can take many forms. You can roll on or choose a result from the Unlight Manifestation table to inspire the form your Radiant Rage takes.

1d6Your Unlight Manifests As…
1White light blazing from your eyes and mouth.
2Complex arcane symbols of light etched onto your body.
3A cyclone of bright light that whirls around you.
4Cracks in your flesh that reveal burning light within.
5A ghostly form made of light that hovers above you.
6Pale fire that dances on the tip of your weapon.

3rd Level - Radiant Rage​

The Unlight coursing through you fuels your Rage. While your Rage is active, you shed Bright Light in a 15-foot radius. Additionally, if a creature damages or hits you with an attack roll while within your area of Bright Light, the creature takes Radiant damage equal to twice your Rage Damage bonus.

6th Level - Unlight Revelation​

You have proficiency in the Perception skill, if you lack it. You also gain Expertise in that skill.

While your rage is active, you have Blindsight with a range equal to that of the Bright Light provided by your Radiant Rage.

10th Level - Infectious Unlight​

Damage you deal with Brutal Strike can be Radiant or the usual type (your choice). The following effect is now among your Brutal Strike options.

Radiant Infection. The target becomes infected with Unlight for 1 minute. While infected, it sheds Bright Light in a 10-foot radius and gains no benefit from being hidden or invisible. Additionally, at the start of each of its turns roll a 1d12; it takes that much in Radiant damage and also takes a penalty on the first attack roll and Constitution saving throw it makes before the start of your next turn equal to the amount rolled. The target can use an Action to purge the Infection from their system, ending the effect on a success.

10th Level - Harbinger of Unlight​

You have Resistance to Radiant damage.

14th Level - Brilliant Rage​

The aura of your Unlight is bolstered. While your Rage is active, you shed Bright Light in a 30-foot radius.

As a Bonus Action, you unleash your blinding radiance. When you do, each creature of your choice within 30 feet of you makes a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 plus your Strength modifier and Proficiency Bonus).

On a failed save, roll a number of d12's equal to your Rage Damage bonus. The target takes that much in Radiant damage and half as much radiant damage again at the start of their turn. Additionally they can see no further than 5ft in any direction until the end of their next turn. On a successful save they take half as much initial damage and no damage at the start of their turn, and suffer no effect to their vision.

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Strength modifier, resetting on a Long Rest or if you expend a use of your Rage (no action required) to restore one use of it.
