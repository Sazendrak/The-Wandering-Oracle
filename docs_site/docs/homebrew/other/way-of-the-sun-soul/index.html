Way of the Sun Soul

Way of the Sun Soul​

Monk Subclass — D&D 5e (2014) (HB) Homebrew Revision

Sun Soul monks channel their ki into searing light and solar flame, empowering their strikes, projecting radiant energy, and eventually embodying the brilliance of the sun.

Features:

  • 3rd Level - Radiant Sun Soul
  • 6th Level - Blinding Radiance
  • 11th Level - Solar Ascension
  • 17th Level - Radiance Incarnate

3rd Level - Radiant Sun Soul​

Radiant Sun Bolt​

Whenever you make an unarmed strike as part of your Attack action, Martial Arts, or Flurry of Blows, you can replace that attack with a Radiant Sun Bolt.

Make a ranged spell attack using Dexterity as your spellcasting ability against a creature within 60 feet. You are proficient with this attack. On a hit, the target takes radiant damage equal to your Martial Arts die + your Dexterity modifier.

Searing Strike​

Your unarmed strikes can deal radiant damage instead of their normal damage type.

Solar Radiance​

You can cause your body to shed bright light in a 10-foot emanation and dim light for an additional 10 feet. You can activate or suppress this light at will (no action required).

6th Level - Blinding Radiance​

Blinding Flare​

After you hit a creature with a radiant unarmed strike or Radiant Sun Bolt, you can use your bonus action and spend 1 ki point to force it to make a Constitution saving throw against your ki save DC.

On a failure, the creature is blinded until the end of your next turn. On a success, it is unaffected.

11th Level - Solar Ascension​

Solar Flight​

When you use Step of the Wind, you gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed and can hover until the end of your current turn.

Searing Sunburst​

As an action, you create an explosion of radiant energy centered on a point you can see within 120 feet.

Creatures of your choice within a 20-foot-radius sphere must make a Constitution saving throw against your ki save DC.

On a failed save, a creature takes radiant damage equal to four Martial Arts dice, is pushed up to 15 feet away from the center, and suffers Solar Burn.

On a successful save, it takes half as much radiant damage and suffers no additional effects.

Solar Burn. The target takes 1d6 fire damage at the start of each of its turns for up to 3 rounds. It can use a bonus action to extinguish the flames. Multiple applications refresh the duration instead of stacking.

You can use Searing Sunburst once without spending ki, regaining that use when you finish a long rest. Additional uses cost 3 ki points each.

17th Level - Radiance Incarnate​

As a bonus action, you can spend 5 ki points to embody the full brilliance of the sun for 1 minute. The transformation ends early if you’re incapacitated or choose to end it (no action required).

While transformed, you gain the following benefits:

Solar Flight. You gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed and can hover.

Blazing Soul. Once on each of your turns when you hit with an unarmed strike or Radiant Sun Bolt, you deal additional radiant damage equal to two Martial Arts dice.

Sun Shield. When a creature within 10 feet of you hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to deal radiant damage equal to two Martial Arts dice.

Eyes of the Sun. You have advantage on saving throws against effects that would impose the blinded condition.

Wrath of the Sun. Once during the transformation, you can use your action to unleash a wave of solar energy. Creatures of your choice within 30 feet must make a Dexterity saving throw against your ki save DC.

On a failed save, a creature takes radiant damage equal to five Martial Arts dice and falls prone. On a successful save, it takes half as much damage and isn’t knocked prone.
