Pugilist Class

The Purity Rule: This cycle must have consisted entirely of levels from one of the following classes and subclasses, with no multiclassing:

  • Barbarian (Path of the Brawler)

  • Barbarian (Path of the Battlerager)

  • Shinobi (Nindo of the Iron Fist)

  • Wizard (Beat your Ass)

  • Bard (College of Dance)

  • Channeler (Channel of Containment)

  • Bulwark (Unfettered)

  • Hit Die: 1d10 per pugilist level

PL 21: Prestige Scaling​

You have mastered the raw fundamentals of combat. Any feature in this class that refers to your level uses your Prestige Level (PL), which is calculated as L20 + Pugilist Level. Additionally, your Pugilist Save DC for all features is 8 + your Proficiency Bonus + \your choice of Strength or Constitution modifier.

PL21: Fisticuffs​

Your years of fighting in back alleys and taverns have given you mastery over combat styles that use Unarmed Strikes and Pugilist weapons (Simple Melee weapons and Improvised weapons). You gain the following benefits while you are unarmed or wielding only Pugilist weapons, and not wielding a Shield:

  • Bonus Unarmed Strike: You can make one Unarmed Strike as a Bonus Action.
  • Fisticuffs Die: You can roll the die shown in the Fisticuffs column of the Pugilist Table in place of the normal damage of your Unarmed Strike or Pugilist weapons.
  • Improved Improvisation: You have a talent for turning anything into a weapon. All improvised weapons and your unarmed strike gain your choice of the Sap or Topple mastery property for you.

PL 21: Iron Chin​

You have Resistance to Bludgeoning damage, while have resistance to Bludgeoning damage from another source you instead have immunity to bludgeoning damage. Once your Prestige Level reaches 30, this hardiness expands to grant you resistance to Piercing and Slashing damage as well.

PL 22: Moxie​

Your experiences beating the odds have given you a swagger you can channel in battle. This is represented by Moxie Points. You have a maximum number of Moxie Points as shown in the Pugilist Table. You regain all expended Moxie Points only when you finish a Long Rest. You can spend these points to fuel the following features:

  • Brace Up: You can use a Bonus Action and expend 1 Moxie Point to brace for attacks. When you do so, roll your Fisticuffs die and gain a number of Temporary Hit Points equal to the number rolled plus your Prestige Level and your Constitution modifier.
  • One-Two Punch: When you use your Bonus Action to make an Unarmed Strike, you can expend 1 Moxie Point to make two Unarmed Strikes as a Bonus Action instead of one.
  • Stick and Move: You can use a Bonus Action and expend 1 Moxie Point to make one Unarmed Strike and take either the Dash or Disengage action.

PL 22: Bloodied But Unbowed​

When you take damage, you can use your Reaction to gain a surge of adrenaline. You gain Temporary Hit Points equal to six times your Pugilist Level. These Temporary Hit Points vanish if any remain when you finish a Short or Long Rest. Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

PL 22: Swagger Streak​

When you fail a Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, or Charisma check, you can expend 1 Moxie Point to attempt to swagger your way to success. Roll your Fisticuffs die and add the number rolled to the ability check, potentially turning it into a success. If the ability check still fails, you regain the expended Moxie Point.

PL 23: Heavy Hitter​

You have learned to put your whole body into every strike. When you hit a creature with an Unarmed Strike, you can deal damage and use both your choice of the Grapple or Shove options against the target.

PL 23: Pugilist Subclass​

You can choose a pugilist archetype and you gain more features from the subclass on the following levels, PL26, PL31, PL37

Options:

  • Dog and Hound
  • Street Saint
  • Squared Circle
  • Piss and Vinegar

PL 24: Dig Deep​

You can use a Bonus Action to find a hidden reserve of strength. For 1 minute, you have Resistance to Piercing and Slashing damage and you ignore the effects of Exhaustion levels 1 through 5. Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest unless you gain 1 level of Exhaustion (no action required) to restore your use of it.

PL 25: Extra Strike​

Once per turn, when you make an attack with an Pugilist weapon or unarmed strike as part of your action you can make an additional unarmed strike as part of that same action

PL 25: Haymaker​

When you make an attack with an Unarmed Strike or Pugilist weapon, you can expend 3 Moxie Points to swing with wild abandon. On a hit, the attack becomes a Critical Hit. If the attack roll was already a Critical Hit, you instead triple the damage dice for the attack.

PL 26: Moxie-Fueled Fists​

Whenever you deal damage with your Unarmed Strike or an attack with an improvised weapon, the strike is considered magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to non-magical attacks, and you can choose to have it deal Force damage instead of its normal type.

PL 27: Down But Not Out​

While you are Bloodied (at or below half your maximum Hit Points), your spirit flares with desperation. You add a bonus to the damage of your Unarmed Strikes and Pugilist weapons equal to your Constitution modifier plus your current level of Exhaustion.

PL 29: School of Hard Knocks​

Once per turn, you can deal an extra 1d12 damage when you hit with an Unarmed Strike or Pugilist weapon. You can forgo this extra damage to apply one of the following effects:

  • Endanger: The next time the creature is hit by an attack before the start of your next turn, the attack deals its maximum possible damage rather than rolling, this only applies to the pugilist or Unarmed strike damage, and not with any external damage riders
  • Provoke: The creature has Disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you until the end of your next turn.

PL 30: Herculean​

Your physical prowess reaches legendary proportions. You gain the following benefits:

  • Heavy Lifter: Your Strength score is doubled when determining your carrying capacity and the weight you can push, drag, or lift.
  • Pillar Breaker: When you hit an object or structure with an Unarmed Strike, the hit is an automatic Critical Hit.
  • Unmatched Athlete: Your jump distance is doubled.

PL 30: Shake It Off​

At the start of each of your turns (no action required), you can expend 2 Moxie Points to remove one level of Exhaustion from yourself or end one of the following conditions on yourself: Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, Restrained, or Stunned.

PL 33: Dig Deeper​

Your resolve is bottomless. While your Dig Deep feature is active, you gain the following additional benefits:

  • You can use your School of Hard Knocks feature twice per turn instead of once.
  • You can attack three times, instead of twice, whenever you use one-two punch on your turn.

PL 34: Unbreakable​

You have Advantage on Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution saving throws. Additionally, whenever you make a saving throw and fail, you can expend 1 Moxie Point to reroll it, and you must use the new roll.

PL 35: Pugnacious​

When you roll Initiative, you can choose to remove one level of Exhaustion from yourself and regain your expended uses of Bloodied But Unbowed and Dig Deep. Once you use this feature, you cannot do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

When you roll Initiative and have no uses of moxie left, you regain an amount equal to your Constitution Modifier.

PL 38: Fighting Spirit​

When you are reduced to 0 Hit Points but not killed outright, you can drop to 1 Hit Point instead. When you do, you gain Temporary Hit Points equal to double your Prestige Level , and you have Resistance to all damage except Force for the next minute. While these Temporary Hit Points remain, you are immune to the Stunned and Paralyzed conditions. Once you use this feature, you can’t use it again until you finish a Long Rest.

PL 40: Peak Physical Condition​

You have reached the absolute zenith of mortal capability. You gain the following benefits:

  • Hale and Hearty: You gain a permanent +2 to your AC while not wielding a shield and wearing only light armor or no armor, and your hit points increases by 40
  • Sleep It Off: When you finish a Long Rest, you lose all levels of Exhaustion rather than just one.
  • Vim and Vigor: When you finish a Short Rest, you regain Hit Points equal to three times your Prestige Level.
