Ranger Wildtrak

Prerequisites​

Level 20 Cycle: You must have completed 20 levels of Ranger (X's Revised Again Ranger) with no multiclassing.

Hit Dice: 1d10 per level

PL 21: Proficiencies​

When you take this prestige class, choose two additional skills from the Ranger skill list. You gain proficiency in both.

PL 21: Ascendant Spellcasting​

You gain a new set of spell slots to fuel your spells. You gain spell slots as per the Ranger table based on your Prestige Level minus 20. Your number of spells known equals 11 plus the Spells Known entry on the Ranger table for your Prestige Level minus 20, as shown in the Spells Known column above.

Because Ranger spellcasting begins at 2nd level, your new slots and spells begin at PL 22.

PL 21: Warden's Ascension​

Your Warden Specialization reaches beyond mortal limits. You gain features based on your specialization at PL 21, 26, and 34, as detailed in the Warden's Ascension section below.

PL 22: Studied Quarry​

When you designate your Favored Foe, you learn its damage resistances, immunities, vulnerabilities, and condition immunities, plus its highest and lowest saving throws.

Once per turn when you hit your Favored Foe with a weapon attack, you can change the damage type of that hit to acid, cold, fire, lightning, or poison.

PL 23: Expanded Ranger Archetype​

Your talents expand. You may choose the same Ranger archetype, or a new one to broaden your craft. Your choice grants you features at PL 23, 27, 33, and 37.

PL 24: Ability Score Improvement​

At PL 24, 28, 32, 36, and 39, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or two ability scores of your choice by 1. As normal, you can't increase an ability score above 20 using this feature. You may select a Feat instead.

PL 25: Advanced Fighting Style​

When you reach 25th level your previous fighting styles grow to fit your new strengths. Any fighting style you gained through the ranger class or ranger archetype is improved in the following ways:

Advanced Archery​

You have a +2 bonus to attack rolls with ranged weapons. Your ranged weapons also deal one additional damage die.

Advanced Defense​

While you are wearing armor, you gain a +2 bonus to AC and saving throws.

Advanced Druidic Warrior​

You add your Wisdom modifier to the damage of any cantrip learned through Druidic Warrior.

Advanced Dueling​

When you are wielding a melee weapon in one hand and no other weapons, you gain a +2 bonus to damage rolls with that weapon. Additionally, creatures you hit with that weapon have disadvantage to hit anyone other than you until the start of your next turn.

Advanced Great-Weapon Fighting​

When you roll a 1 or a 2 on a damage die for an attack roll you make with a melee weapon that you are wielding in two hands, you can reroll the damage die. You may reroll the damage die if the result is a 1 or a 2 as many times as you like. The weapon must have the two-handed or versatile property to gain this benefit.

Advanced Protection​

When a creature you can see attacks a target other than you that is within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to cause that attack roll to automatically miss.

Advanced Two-Weapon Fighting​

When you engage in two-weapon fighting, you can make one additional attack with the second weapon as part of the same bonus action, adding your ability modifier to both if it does not already.

PL 27: Swift Hunter (Prestige)​

The speed bonus from your Swift Hunter feature increases to 20 feet. Additionally, while your Favored Foe lives and you are in combat with it, you can take the Dash action as a Bonus Action.

PL 29: Peerless Hunter (Prestige)​

When you have Advantage on an attack roll against your Favored Foe, you roll three d20s instead of two and use the highest result. This also applies to any check you make to locate your Favored Foe.

PL 30: Primeval Dominion​

Your Primeval Awareness now has a range of 50 miles, and you can also choose beasts, giants, monstrosities, and plants. You learn the number of creatures of the chosen type and the direction to each one.

While Primeval Awareness is active, you and allies within 30 feet of you can't be surprised by creatures of the chosen type, and you have Advantage on Initiative rolls.

PL 31: Hunter's Harrying​

When you hit your Favored Foe, it can't take Reactions until the start of your next turn, and its speed is reduced by 10 feet until then. This speed reduction can stack up to three times, reducing the speed by up to 30 feet.

PL 33: Unseen Stalker​

When you use Nature's Veil, the invisibility lasts for 1 minute, or until you make two attack rolls or cast a spell. The number of times you can use Nature's Veil equals your Proficiency Bonus plus your Wisdom modifier.

PL 35: Deadly Vigil​

You can make the Opportunity Attacks your Favored Foe provokes with a ranged weapon. The attack triggers when your Favored Foe leaves a space within 30 feet of you, or when it takes an action that would normally avoid provoking you.

When you hit your Favored Foe with an Opportunity Attack, its speed becomes 0 until the end of its turn.

PL 38: Apex Predator​

Once per round, you can make the Opportunity Attack your Favored Foe provokes without using your Reaction.

Additionally, once per round when your Favored Foe misses you with an attack roll, you can make one weapon attack against it (no action required).

PL 40: Final Quarry​

Your Foe Slayer feature now adds twice your Wisdom modifier to damage rolls against your Favored Foe. When you reduce your Favored Foe to 0 hit points, you regain hit points equal to your Prestige Level and one use of Nature's Veil.

Additionally, your Wisdom score increases by 4, to a maximum of 30.

Warden's Ascension​

Iron Warden​

PL 21: Ironclad. While you are wearing Heavy Armor, the bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage you take is reduced by your Wisdom modifier. You also have Advantage on saving throws against being knocked Prone or moved against your will.

PL 26: Unyielding Ward. When a creature within 5 feet of you makes an attack against a target other than you, you can use your Reaction to make one weapon attack against it. On a hit, the triggering attack has Disadvantage.

PL 34: Mountain's Endurance. While you are wearing Heavy Armor, you have Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage. Additionally, when you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you drop to 1 hit point instead. Once you use this benefit, you can't do so again until you finish a Long Rest.

Earthen Warden​

PL 21: Stone Shadow. You can take the Hide action as a Bonus Action. If you miss with an attack while hidden, you remain hidden.

PL 26: Earthmeld. You gain a burrow speed equal to your walking speed. You can move through nonmagical earth and stone without disturbing the material you move through, and your tremorsense increases to 60 feet.

PL 34: Unseen Predator. Your blindsight increases to 120 feet. Once per turn, when you hit a creature while hidden, you remain hidden if you are not within 5 feet of it.

Nature's Warden​

PL 21: Primal Channel. You learn two additional cantrips from the Druid spell list. Additionally, when you learn a spell from Ascendant Spellcasting, you can choose it from the Druid spell list, and it counts as a Ranger spell for you. When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can replace one of the attacks with casting a druid cantrip you know.

PL 26: Verdant Arcana. You learn one 7th-level spell from the Druid spell list. This spell counts as a Ranger spell for you. You can cast it once without expending a spell slot, regaining the ability to do so when you finish a Long Rest.

PL 34: Heart of the Wild. You learn one 8th-level spell from the Druid spell list under the same terms as Verdant Arcana. Additionally, you regain the use of the 6th-level spell from your Warden's Pinnacle when you finish a Short Rest.
