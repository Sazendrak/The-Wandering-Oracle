Justiciar

Description​

The oath was never the end of it. It was the apprenticeship.

Prerequisites: Paladin 20

As a Justiciar, you gain the following features.

Proficiencies​

You gain proficiency in two skills of your choice from the Paladin skill list.

Ascendant Spellcasting​

Additionally when you take this prestige class, you gain an increased capacity for spellcasting. You now use half your prestige level (rounded up) on the generic spellcaster level table when determining the amount of spell slots you have available. When you prepare spells for the day, you may now prepare a number of spells equal to half your prestige level + your Charisma modifier.

When preparing spells, you now have access to the Cleric spell list for your 6th- to 9th-level spells. You must have spell slots of the spell's level in order to prepare it as normal.

Righteous Cantrip​

At 21st level, you learn one cantrip of your choice from the Cleric spell list. These cantrips count as Paladin/Justiciar spells for you, and Charisma is your spellcasting ability for them.

Divine Sense II​

At 21st level, you gain twice the number of uses of your Divine Sense feature.

Lay on Hands II​

At 21st level, your blessed touch can heal wounds better than before. Your pool of healing power is instead a total number that is now equal to your prestige level x 5.

As an action, you can touch a creature and draw power from that pool to restore a number of hit points to that creature, up to a maximum amount remaining in your pool. When expending the last point in your healing pool to do so, it will heal that creature by the maximum number of hit dice that creature has. Additionally, in exchange for one of your attacks during an Attack action you may use Lay on Hands targeting yourself only; doing it this way takes 2 points per 1 hit point healed.

Alternatively, you can expend 5 hit points from your pool of healing to cure the target of one disease or neutralize one poison affecting it. You can cure multiple diseases and neutralize multiple poisons with a single use of Lay on Hands II, expending hit points separately for each one.

Alternatively, you can expend 10 hit points from your pool of healing to cure the target of one condition affecting it. You can cure multiple conditions with a single use of Lay on Hands, expending hit points separately for each one.

Alternatively, you can expend 20 points to remove 1 level of exhaustion from a willing creature.

Godly Smite​

Starting at 22nd level, this feature replaces your Paladin's 2nd-level Divine Smite feature.

When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can expend one spell slot to deal radiant damage to the target, in addition to the weapon's damage. The extra damage is now 3d8 for a 1st-level spell slot, plus 1d8 for each spell level higher than 1st, to a maximum of 8d8. The damage increases by 3d8 if the target is an undead or a fiend, to a maximum of 11d8.

This feature counts as Divine Smite for prerequisites and interactions with other class or subclass features.

Everlasting Oath​

When you reach 23rd level, you reaffirm your prior oath and swear another oath that binds you as a Justiciar forever. Up to this time, you have been faithfully carrying out your former oath. Now you rededicate yourself to your former oath, and swear another paladin's oath. Choose from the list of available oaths.

Your choice grants you features at 23rd, 27th, 35th, and 40th level.

Divine Health II​

By 23rd level, the divine magic flowing through you makes you immune to the Poisoned condition and grants you resistance to poison damage.

Channel Divinity​

At 23rd level, you gain one additional use of your Channel Divinity per rest.

Ability Score Improvement​

When you reach 24th level, you gain the Ability Score Improvement feat or another feat of your choice for which you qualify. You gain this feature again at Justiciar levels 28, 32, 36, and 39.

Extra Attack (3)​

At 25th level, you can attack three times instead of twice whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

Aura of the Guardian​

At 26th level, the range of your Aura of the Guardian extends to 40 feet.

Aura Expansion​

At 27th level, the range of all your Paladin subclass auras extends to 40 feet.

Aura of Bravery​

At 30th level, creatures retain the effects of your Aura of Courage for 1 round after leaving it.

At 38th level, this extends to 1 minute after leaving your aura.

Improved Divine Smite II​

By 31st level, you are so suffused with righteous might that all your melee weapon strikes carry divine power with them. Whenever you hit a creature with a melee weapon, the creature takes an extra 2d10 radiant damage instead of 1d8.

Warding Touch​

At 34th level, you can, as a reaction, reflect 1st- and 2nd-level spells back at their caster for a charge of Cleansing Touch. For each additional charge used, this increases the maximum level by 2, up to a maximum of 8th level. This use of Cleansing Touch is a reaction.

You may now use your Cleansing Touch feature as a bonus action, and you gain a number of uses of that feature equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of one).

Aura Improvements II​

At 38th level, extend the range of any and all auras from your Paladin/Justiciar class, or its subclasses, to 50 feet.

Avatar State​

At 40th level, when you activate one of your 20th-level subclass features that requires activation, you may also activate your other 20th-level subclass feature (should it require activation). Both last for the longer of the two features' durations.
