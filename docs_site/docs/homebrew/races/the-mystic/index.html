The Mystic

Psionic Prestige Class

Every psi-adept eventually finds the same wall. The soulknife learns to sharpen thought into a blade but never learns what else thought can be. The psi warrior learns to shove the world with their mind but never learns to unmake it. The wall is not a limit of power — it is a limit of vocabulary. The Mystic is what happens when a mortal stops using the gift as a tool and begins speaking the language it was cut from.

Prerequisites​

Level 20 Cycle. You must have completed a full 20-level paragon cycle satisfying the Purity Rule.

The Purity Rule. That cycle must have consisted entirely of levels from one of the following, with no multiclassing:

  • Rogue (Soulknife)
  • Fighter (Psi Warrior)
  • Sorcerer (Aberrant Mind)
  • Warlock (Great Old One Patron)

Hit Die: 1d8 per Mystic level

Your entry path determines your Order. You do not choose it.

Design Principle​

This class is additive. You already have a combat loop that works — Sneak Attack, Flurry of Blows, Extra Attack, Metamagic, Eldritch Blast. The Mystic does not replace it and should never compete with it for your Action.

Almost every discipline is a Bonus Action, a Reaction, or a rider on something you were already doing. The handful that cost a full Magic action are Ascendant-band effects meant for the turn you decide to end an encounter.

Your Manifestation Dice are a separate pool from any psionic resource you already had. The two never convert. Your Order deepens the old pool across five growth points; the class runs the new one.

PL 21 Core Features: Prestige Scaling, Proficiencies, Psionic Ability, Psionics, Manifestation, Psychic Focus, Separate Pools.

The Bands column shows the highest band you can activate. It includes every band below it.

PL 21: Manifestation​

At the start of each of your turns, you can expend any number of your Manifestation Dice, rolling them all at once. The total is your Manifested Psi for the round.

That psi is available until the start of your next turn, including during other creatures' turns. Any left over is lost.

Generating psi costs no action. Expending dice is one decision, made once per round, before you know what the round holds.

You regain half your expended Manifestation Dice (rounded up) on a Short Rest, and all of them on a Long Rest.

Manifesting outside combat​

When you aren't in initiative, there are no turns to key off. You can expend Manifestation Dice at any time with no action; the resulting psi lasts 1 minute or until spent, whichever comes first. Any effect you pay for runs its full written duration regardless.

Rolling a die vs expending one​

Two phrases appear throughout this class and they mean different things:

  • "A roll of your Manifestation Die" refers only to the size of the die on the table — d8, d10, or d12. You roll one and use the number. Nothing is expended. Your pool is untouched.
  • "Expend a die" means the die leaves your pool and must be recovered on a rest.

So a feature that adds a roll of your Manifestation Die to damage costs you nothing and works every round forever. A feature that says expend is spending a resource. The same distinction applies to Psionic Energy Dice.

There is no per-activation cap to track. If you can afford it and the band is unlocked, you can use it.

Psi is the only thing you spend. A psychic focus costs nothing and runs continuously, so a turn where you expend no dice still has psionics in it — just passive ones.

The trophy rule​

Any feature that rewards you for reducing a creature to 0 Hit Points triggers only on creatures of Challenge Rating 1 or higher that you did not summon, create, or command. This covers Relentless, Feed, Siphon, Warped Blade, and anything like them. Minions are not a resource farm.

The one-spell rule​

No feature of this class ever lets you concentrate on, or maintain, more than one spell at a time. Disciplines can stack with each other and with a spell where a feature allows it, but a second spell always ends the first. This holds regardless of Fractured Concentration, Psionic Mastery, or any combination of them.

What a turn actually looks like​

A PL 26 Whetstone Soulknife. 8d10 Manifestation Dice.

Start of turn. She expends one die, rolls a 6. 6 psi for the round. Action. Attack action with Psychic Blades, as normal. Sneak Attack, as normal. On the hit she folds in Mindspike's Lance — 3 psi, no action — for an extra 3d8 and an Intelligence save. 3 left. Bonus Action. Cunning Action to Disengage, exactly as she always did. Someone else's turn. The wizard gets targeted. She spends her Reaction on Intellect Fortress's Ward, 3 psi. 0 left. Start of her next turn. The 3 evaporates. She decides again.

PL 21: Prestige Scaling​

Any feature referring to your level uses your Prestige Level (PL) = 20 + your Mystic level.

Discipline save DC = 8 + Proficiency Bonus + Psionic Ability modifier. Discipline attack modifier = Proficiency Bonus + Psionic Ability modifier.

PL 21: Proficiencies​

Choose two additional skills from Arcana, History, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Perception, and Religion.

PL 21: Psionic Ability​

Choose Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma. That ability powers every feature of this class. The choice is permanent, alterable only by retraining, and does not change the ability used by your entry subclass.

Soulknives may instead choose Dexterity — see The Whetstone.

PL 21: Psionics​

You know the number of disciplines shown on the table, chosen from the Shared Disciplines list. Your Order grants two Order Disciplines that don't count against this number and that no other Order can learn. On each level in this class you can swap one discipline for another.

PL 21: Psychic Focus​

As a Bonus Action, choose one discipline you know. You gain that discipline's ○ Focus benefit, which lasts until you are Incapacitated or until you use a Bonus Action to focus on a different discipline.

Focus costs nothing. It is not Concentration — it can't be broken by damage, and it doesn't interfere with a spell or discipline you are concentrating on.

Focus and manifestation are independent. Focusing on a discipline does not let you activate its options for free, and activating a discipline's options does not require you to be focused on it. You can be focused on Iron Skin for the AC while spending all your psi on Mindspike, and you can activate any option of any discipline you know regardless of what you are focused on.

In practice you set a focus once at the start of a fight and leave it. Switching costs a Bonus Action, so mid-combat changes should be a deliberate call — dropping the Stealth focus to pick up +1 AC when the fighting starts, for instance.

PL 21: Separate Pools​

Manifestation Dice are their own resource. They are not Psionic Energy Dice, Focus Points, Sorcery Points, or spell slots, and never convert into each other in either direction. Your entry subclass's resource continues to work exactly as written, on its own recovery schedule — and your Order deepens it at each of its five growth points.

PL 22: Mystical Recovery​

Once per Short or Long Rest, as a Bonus Action, you can expend any number of Manifestation Dice and regain Hit Points equal to the total rolled, rather than gaining psi from them.

This is the mirror of Consumptive Power: that feature turns blood into thought, this one turns thought into blood. Neither can pay for the other — Mystical Recovery spends dice, not psi, so psi gained from Consumptive Power can never be converted back into Hit Points.

PL 22: Ascendant Telepathy​

You can speak telepathically to any creature you can see within 1 mile, and to any creature you've previously touched or linked with on the same plane. Language is unnecessary, though the creature must understand at least one language or be telepathic. You can maintain links with up to your Psionic Ability modifier in willing creatures, letting them speak to each other through you.

PL 23: Strength of Mind​

After each Long Rest, choose two ability scores. You gain saving throw proficiency in them until your next Long Rest. If you are already proficient in a chosen ability, add half your Proficiency Bonus again to those saves instead.

PL 24: Ability Score Improvement​

At PL 24, 28, 32, 36, and 39. Increase one score by 2 or two by 1, max 20 unless stated otherwise, or take a Feat.

PL 24: Potent Psionics​

Once on each of your turns when you hit with a weapon or Unarmed Strike, or when you deal damage with a spell or discipline, deal an extra 2d8 Psychic damage, rising to 3d8 at PL 35.

PL 26: Die Minimum​

When you roll a Manifestation Die, treat any result below 2 as a 2. This rises to 3 at PL 33 and 4 at PL 38.

PL 26: Consumptive Power​

At any point during your turn, you can spend up to twice your Proficiency Bonus in Hit Points to gain an equal amount of Manifested Psi. No action required. Your current and maximum Hit Points are both reduced by that amount; the reduction to your maximum can't be lessened and lasts until a Long Rest.

Usable a number of times per Long Rest equal to your Proficiency Bonus. This is your correction when you underestimated the round.

PL 27: Unbound Focus​

You can maintain the psychic focus of two disciplines at once, gaining both benefits. Setting a focus while two are active replaces one of your choice. It still costs one Bonus Action to change either.

PL 30: Overchannel​

Once per turn when you activate a discipline, choose one:

  • Amplify. The damage dice of that manifestation are maximized.
  • Widen. Double the area or range of that manifestation, or add one target within its range.
  • Entrench. Double the duration of that manifestation.

Overchannel only ever affects the discipline you are activating. It does not touch weapon damage, Sneak Attack, Martial Arts dice, spell dice, Psionic Energy Dice, or any other damage in the same attack or turn — only the dice printed in the manifestation itself.

You take Psychic damage equal to the psi spent, which can't be reduced or prevented, and have Disadvantage on Constitution saves for concentration until the end of your next turn. Overchannel can't be applied to Ascendant-band options.

PL 31: Fractured Concentration​

Concentrate on two disciplines at once, provided both were paid with Manifested Psi. You can't concentrate on a spell while doing so — see the one-spell rule. On a Constitution save to maintain, roll once with Disadvantage; failure loses both.

PL 34: Psionic Mastery​

Once per Long Rest, at the start of your turn, you can gain Manifested Psi equal to your Mystic level — not your Prestige Level — without expending any dice. At PL 34 that is 14 psi, rising to 20 at PL 40. Until the start of your next turn, you can concentrate on any number of disciplines paid for with that psi simultaneously; concentrating on anything else ends them all.

PL 38: Psionic Body​

  • Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing; immunity to Psychic damage.
  • You no longer age; immune to disease, Poison damage, and the Poisoned condition.
  • No need to eat, drink, or breathe.
  • If you would die, roll a d20. On a 10+ you discorporate instead, vanishing with your gear at 0 Hit Points and reappearing at a place of your choice 1d3 days later on that plane, with the benefits of a Long Rest.

PL 40: Transcendent Mind​

  • Inexhaustible. Once per Short or Long Rest, at the start of your turn, if you have no unspent Manifestation Dice, regain a number equal to half your Proficiency Bonus, rounded up.
  • Perfect Manifestation. Once per Long Rest, when you expend Manifestation Dice at the start of your turn, treat every one of them as its maximum. This applies only to Manifestation Dice rolled for psi — not to damage dice, Psionic Energy Dice, or any other roll.
  • Untouchable Will. Immune to the Charmed and Frightened conditions, to thought-reading and lie detection, and to possession or memory alteration.
  • Mastery Redoubled. You gain a second use of Psionic Mastery.

Shared Disciplines​

Each discipline has one ○ Focus benefit and several paid options. The Focus applies only while you are focused on that discipline (see PL 21: Psychic Focus); the paid options can be used any time, focused or not. Saves are against your Discipline save DC. Action cost is listed on every option.

Rider means no action at all — you fold it into an attack or spell you were already making.

Most manifestations do not require Concentration. Where one does, it is marked.

Mindspike​

○ Focus. Your psionic damage ignores Resistance to Psychic damage.

● Lance (3 psi) — Rider. Once per turn when you hit a creature or damage it with a spell, it makes an Intelligence save, taking 3d8 Psychic damage on a failure, half on a success.

●● Shatter (8 psi) — Bonus Action. One creature within 60 ft makes an Intelligence save, taking 8d8 Psychic damage and being Incapacitated until the end of your next turn on a failure, half damage on a success.

●●● Erase (13 psi) — Magic action. One creature within 60 ft makes an Intelligence save. Failure: 16d8 Psychic damage and Stunned for 1 minute, save repeats at end of each of its turns. Success: half damage.

Kinetic Shove​

○ Focus. Once per turn when you hit with a weapon or spell attack, push or pull the target 10 ft.

● Thrust (2 psi) — Rider. Once per turn when you hit, the target makes a Strength save or is pushed 20 ft and knocked Prone.

●● Wave (7 psi) — Bonus Action. Each creature in a 30-ft cone makes a Strength save, taking 6d8 Force damage and being pushed 30 ft and knocked Prone on a failure, half damage on a success.

Iron Skin​

○ Focus. +1 AC.

● Brace (3 psi) — Reaction, when you take damage: halve it.

●● Adamant (8 psi) — Bonus Action. For 1 minute you have Resistance to all damage and can't be moved against your will or knocked Prone. No concentration.

Blink Step​

○ Focus. You don't provoke Opportunity Attacks.

● Step (2 psi) — Bonus Action. Teleport up to 30 ft to a space you can see.

●● Slip (6 psi) — Reaction, when you're targeted by an attack or forced to make a save: teleport up to 120 ft to a space you can see, bringing one willing creature within 5 ft. If this moves you out of the effect, you're unaffected.

Veil​

○ Focus. Advantage on Stealth checks.

● Fade (4 psi) — Bonus Action. You're Invisible for 1 minute. Concentration.

●● Unseen (8 psi) — Bonus Action. As Fade, no concentration, and you can't be detected by divination magic, blindsight, tremorsense, or truesight.

Read the Thread​

○ Focus. You can't be surprised, and you add your Psionic Ability modifier to initiative.

● Foresee (3 psi) — Reaction, when a creature within 60 ft makes an attack roll or save: it has Disadvantage.

●● Rewrite (7 psi) — Reaction, after any d20 test within 60 ft: the roller rerolls and must use the new result. A Critical Hit rerolled this way is not a Critical Hit.

Dominate Impulse​

○ Focus. Advantage on Persuasion, Intimidation, and Deception.

● Compel (4 psi) — Bonus Action. One creature within 60 ft makes a Wisdom save or follows a one-word command on its next turn, as Command.

●● Seize (9 psi) — Bonus Action. One creature within 60 ft makes a Wisdom save or is Charmed and controlled for 1 minute, as Dominate Monster. Concentration.

●●● Enthrone (14 psi) — Magic action. As Seize, up to three creatures. Concentration.

Mend Form​

○ Focus. At the end of a Short Rest, you and each ally who rested with you regain Hit Points equal to your Psionic Ability modifier.

● Knit (4 psi) — Bonus Action. One creature within 30 ft regains 6d8 Hit Points.

●● Restore (8 psi) — Bonus Action. One creature within 30 ft regains 12d8 Hit Points, and you end one condition on it from Blinded, Deafened, Paralyzed, Poisoned, or Stunned.

Terror​

○ Focus. Creatures Frightened of you have Disadvantage on saves against your disciplines.

● Dread (3 psi) — Rider. Once per turn when you damage a creature, it makes a Wisdom save or is Frightened of you for 1 minute, repeating at the end of each of its turns.

●● Panic (7 psi) — Bonus Action. Each creature in a 30-ft cone makes a Wisdom save, taking 5d8 Psychic damage and being Frightened for 1 minute on a failure. No concentration.

Warp Weight​

○ Focus. You can move Small or smaller unattended objects within 30 ft at will, speed 20 ft.

● Grip (3 psi) — Bonus Action. One creature within 60 ft makes a Strength save or is Restrained until the end of your next turn. No concentration.

●● Hurl (7 psi) — Magic action. Lift a creature or object of Huge size or smaller within 60 ft and throw it 60 ft. A thrown creature takes 6d10 Bludgeoning damage, as does anything it lands on.

Second Skin​

○ Focus. Darkvision 60 ft; you can breathe in any environment.

● Adapt (3 psi) — Bonus Action. For 1 hour, climbing and swimming speeds equal to your walking speed, and Advantage on saves against environmental hazards. No concentration.

●● Take Wing (6 psi) — Bonus Action. Flying speed 60 ft for 10 minutes. No concentration.

Intellect Fortress​

○ Focus. Advantage on Constitution saves to maintain concentration.

● Ward (3 psi) — Reaction, when you or an ally within 30 ft takes damage: reduce it by 3d8 plus your Psionic Ability modifier.

●● Bastion (8 psi) — Bonus Action. For 1 minute, you and allies within 10 ft have Resistance to Psychic and Force damage and Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saves. No concentration.

Cascade of Selves (Ascendant)​

○ Focus. Once per turn, when you make a d20 test, roll twice and use either result.

●●● Splinter (12 psi) — Bonus Action. Three duplicates of you appear within 5 ft for 1 minute, moving with you as a Reaction. You can make attacks and target spells from any duplicate's position. When a creature attacks you, roll a d4; on a 2+ a duplicate is struck instead and destroyed. No concentration.

●●● Divergent Turn (14 psi) — Magic action. Take an additional turn immediately after this one. You gain no Manifested Psi at the start of that turn, and once-per-turn features you already used are not refreshed. You can't use this option again until you finish a Long Rest.

Unmake (Ascendant)​

○ Focus. Your weapon attacks and psionic damage ignore Resistance.

●●● Erasure (12 psi) — Magic action. One creature or object within 60 ft. A creature makes a Constitution save, taking 14d10 Force damage on a failure, half on a success. A creature killed by this leaves nothing and returns only via True Resurrection or Wish. A nonmagical object of Large size or smaller is destroyed outright.

●●● Unwrite (13 psi) — Magic action. End one magical effect of 8th level or lower within 60 ft, permanently. A magic item property ends until repaired, 1 week has passed.

Mystic Orders​

Every Order has five growth points — PL 21, 25, 29, 33, and 37. Each carries a feature and a deepening of the pool you arrived with. PL 21 also grants your two Order Disciplines, detailed on each Order's page.

Growth rates. Your entry bump is proportional to your original pool: a third for Psionic Energy Dice, which are a subclass resource of twelve, and a quarter for Focus and Sorcery Points, which are full class resources of twenty. Every growth point after that adds a flat amount.
