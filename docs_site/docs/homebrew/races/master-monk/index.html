Class Features

PL 21: Proficiencies​

When you take this prestige class, choose two additional skills from the Monk skill list. You gain proficiency in both.

PL 21: Ascendant Martial Arts​

Your training has transcended mortal limits. Your Martial Arts die is now 1d12.

PL 22: Uncanny Metabolism (Prestige)​

You can now use your Uncanny Metabolism feature twice per Long Rest, the second time you use it you only recover half of your total focus points. Additionally, when you use it, you regain a number of Hit Points equal to four times your Prestige Level.

PL 23: Perfect Deflection​

You can now use your Deflect Attacks feature against melee, ranged, and energy-based attack rolls. When you do so, you can roll an additional Martial arts die and add the result to the damage reduction. The Deflection damage is increased to 4 martial arts die rolls

PL 23: Expanded Monk Tradition​

Your talents expand, You may choose the same Monk tradition, or you may choose a new one to reflect the different studies and focus learned from another tradition. At PL 23, PL 26, PL 31 and PL37, you gain more features from your Monk tradition.

PL 24: Ability Score Improvement​

At PL 24, 28, 32, 36, and 39, you can increase one ability score of your choice by 2, or two ability scores by 1. As normal, you cannot increase a score above 20 (unless another feature allows it). You may select a Feat instead of an ASI.

PL 25: Relentless Pressure​

When a creature successfully saves against your stunning strike feature, their speed is reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn and they are considered to be dazed for the same duration, your next attack against that creature will have advantage.

Additionally, once per turn when you attack a creature that is already dazed from one of your stunning strike attempts, you can attempt another stunning strike. The target makes the save with disadvantage.

PL 26: Martial Minimum​

Your strikes are consistently devastating. When you roll a Martial Arts die, you treat any roll lower than a 2 as a 2. This minimum increases to 3 at PL 32, and 4 at PL 38.

PL 27: Monk Avoidance​

At 27th level, your monk’s training and mental fortitude allows you to resist assaults on the mind. When you are subjected to an effect that allows you to make a Intelligence or Wisdom saving throw to take only half damage, you instead take no damage if you succeed on the saving throw, and only half damage if you fail.

PL 29: Ascendant Step of the Wind​

When you use your Step of the Wind feature, you gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed until the end of your current turn. Additionally your movement speed increases by 20 feet.

PL 30: Focused Impact​

Any time you roll a Martial Arts die (for damage or features), you can expend 1 Focus Point to roll an additional Martial Arts die and add the result to the first roll.

PL33: Resist Energy​

Whenever you fail a saving throw that deals damage, you can use your perfect deflection ability to reduce the damage. You cannot however deflect the damage back if you reduce the damage to 0.

PL 34: Disciplined Thriver​

Your mental and physical discipline grants you expertise in your defenses. Choose two saving throws in which you are proficient:

  • One Major Save (Dexterity, Constitution, or Wisdom).
  • One Minor Save (Strength, Intelligence, or Charisma). You add your Proficiency Bonus twice to those saving throws instead of once.

PL 35: Fluid Reflexes​

You gain an additional Reaction on every round of combat. This reaction can only be used for Monk class features (such as Deflect Attacks or Slow Fall).

PL 38: Superior Focus​

The cost of your superior defense or empty body is now 3 Focus Points, and you are invisible for the duration.

Additionally, your heightened senses give you blindsight to 30 feet.

PL 40: Peak Perfection​

Your body and soul reach their ultimate form. You gain 6 ability points to distribute between your Dexterity and Wisdom scores as you choose. You can increase the ability scores to a maximum of 30 with this feature.
